# Law of Georgia on State Audit Office

ORGANIC LAW OF GEORGIA

ON STATE AUDIT OFFICE

*Law of Georgia No 6550 of 22 June 2012 - website, 29.6.2012*

## Chapter I - General Provisions

### Article 1 - Purpose of the Law

This Law, in accordance with the Constitution of Georgia, determines the status, powers, structure, rules of procedure and guarantees of independence of the State Audit Office.

### Article 3 - Independence of the State Audit Office

1. The State Audit Office shall be independent in its activities and bound only by the law. Any interference with and/or control over its activities, or any request for a report in connection with such activities, shall be impermissible, unless expressly provided for by law. Any political pressure on the State Audit Office, as well as any other action that may infringe upon its independence, shall be prohibited.

2. The State Audit Office shall be institutionally, financially, functionally and organisationally independent.

### Article 4 – Principal objectives of the State Audit Office

The principal objectives of the activities of the State Audit Office shall be to promote the efficiency and accountability of public governance, to safeguard the legality, purposefulness, and efficiency of the use and expenditure of budgetary funds and other public resources, to protect national wealth and the property of the State, autonomous republics and municipalities, as well as to improve public financial management.

### Article 5 - Legal framework and principles for the activities of the State Audit Office

1. The State Audit Office shall conduct its activities in compliance with the Constitution of Georgia, international agreements and treaties of Georgia, this Law,  
the Organic Law of Georgia on Political Associations of Citizens, the Organic Law of Georgia Election Code of Georgia, the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, the Law of Georgia on the Fight against Corruption, the Law of Georgia on Grants, the Law of Georgia on Transparency of Foreign Influence, the Law of Georgia Foreign Agents Registration Act, and other legislative and subordinate normative acts of Georgia.

2. The State Audit Office, when carrying out auditing activities, shall be guided by the main principles laid down by the International Organisation of Supreme Audit Institutions.

3. The main principles for the operation of the State Audit Office shall be:

a) legality;

b) objectivity and impartiality;

c) independence and political neutrality;

d) publicity;

e) professionalism;

f) safeguarding of secrecy and confidentiality;

g) respect for fundamental human rights.

4. Unless otherwise provided for by this Law, the General Administrative Code of Georgia, except for Chapter III thereof, shall not apply to the auditing activities of the State Audit Office and the activities provided for by the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection.

### Article 6 - Authority of the State Audit Office

1. For the purpose of performing its tasks as provided for by the Constitution of Georgia, the State Audit Office shall:

a) conduct audits within the scope of its competence as established by this Law;

b) be authorised to carry out expert examinations and issue conclusions on draft legislative acts and other normative acts regulating the financial and economic spheres, as well as on programmes intended to be funded from the budgets of the State, autonomous republic or municipalities, or which influence the generation of such funds, as well as to submit to the Parliament of Georgia and other relevant bodies and institutions proposals regarding the issuance and improvement of tax legislation and other legislative and subordinate normative acts;

c) formulate relevant proposals for the prevention, detection, and elimination conflicts of interest in public institutions, issue appropriate recommendations on these matters, and undertake other appropriate measures in this field in accordance with the law;

d) develop and implement programmes on matters of ethics, integrity, and accountability in the public service, as well as review and generalise the practise of compliance with the ethical norms by public servants, and formulate appropriate recommendations;

e) ensure, in accordance with the law, the receipt of asset declarations of officials, control over the completion and submission thereof, as well as their storage, monitoring, and publicity, and the implementation of other appropriate measures in this field;

f) formulate relevant proposals for improving whistleblower protection measures, issue appropriate recommendations on whistleblower protection matters, and undertake other appropriate measures in this field in accordance with the law;

g) monitor, in accordance with the law, the financial activities of political associations of citizens (political parties), members of political parties, electoral subjects, and persons with declared party-political objectives, and undertake other appropriate measures in this field;

h) research and analyse international standards and practices in the field of the fight against corruption, the experience and law of Georgia and other nations, and the approaches, opinions and conclusions of relevant international organisations;

i)  
promote public awareness concerning the issues of the fight against corruption, including through the implementation of appropriate educational activities;

j) monitor, in accordance with the procedure established by the Law of Georgia on Grants and other legislative and subordinate acts of Georgia, the legality of the issuance and receipt of grants;

k) exercise the powers provided for by the Law of Georgia Foreign Agents Registration Act;

l) exercise control over the legality of personal data processing and exercise other powers provided for by the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection;

l¹) exercise the powers provided for by the Law of Georgia on Transparency of Foreign Influence;

m) exercise other powers provided for by the legislation of Georgia.

### Article 8 - System of the State Audit Office

1. The State Audit Office shall be headed by the Auditor General.

2. The State Audit Office shall be a unified, centralised system comprising structural units. The structure of the State Audit Office shall be determined by the Auditor General.

3. A Presidium shall be established within the State Audit Office, the composition and rules of operation of which shall be determined by the Auditor General.3¹. Public Audit Institute shall be a legal entity under public law operating within the governance of the State Audit Office.

4. Issues related to the organisation and operation of the State Audit Office shall be regulated in accordance with this Law and the Regulations of the State Audit Office.

### Article 9 - Auditor General

1. The Auditor General shall be elected by the Parliament of Georgia for a term of 5 years upon his/her nomination by the Chairperson of the Parliament in accordance with the procedures established by the Rules of Procedure of the Parliament of Georgia.

1¹.  
A citizen of Georgia who has no criminal record and has higher education in law, possesses at least 5 years of work experience in the judicial system, law enforcement system and/or the field of human rights protection, and has a high professional and moral reputation, may be elected to the position of the Auditor General.

2. The Auditor General shall not hold any other position or engage in any other remunerated activity, except for pedagogical, academic and/or creative activities.

3. The Auditor General shall not be a member of any political party or engage in any other political activities throughout his/her entire tenure in office.

4. The independence of the Auditor General in the exercise of his/her authority shall be ensured. No one shall have the right to interfere with or influence his/her decisions.

5. The relevant state authorities shall ensure the personal safety of the Auditor General in accordance with the established procedure.

### Article 10 - Authority of the Auditor General

The Auditor General shall:

a) direct the activities of the State Audit Office in accordance with the Constitution of Georgia, this Law and other legislative and subordinate normative acts of Georgia;

b) take decisions on issues relating to the activities of the State Audit Office;

c) approve the structure, the staff list, and the rules of public service within the State Audit Office; determine position titles; appoint and dismiss the officers of the State Audit Office; take decisions on the transfer of an officer to another position; conclude and terminate administrative and employment agreements; determine the procedure for issuance and amounts of remuneration, salary supplements, and monetary rewards, and, in exceptional circumstances, the amount of a lump-sum allowance; take decisions regarding internships, the evaluation of professional official activities, and offering incentives and application of disciplinary measures to officers;;

d) award departmental decorations to the officers of the State Audit Office and nominate them for the conferment of honorary titles and state awards of Georgia;

e) issue a normative act – an order - in accordance with the procedure established by the Law of Georgia on Normative Acts;

f) be empowered to issue an individual administrative act − an order, a decree, an instruction, and also to take a decision for the purpose of exercising his/her powers;

g) be empowered to issue a recommendation in accordance with the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection;

h) approve Rules of Procedure of the State Audit Office, the Code of Ethics for the Personnel, the Internal Regulations, and the statutes of the structural units;

i) determine the powers of the First Deputy Auditor General and Deputy Auditors General and delegate powers to them;

j) be empowered to assign the function of Public Relations Secretary to an officer of the State Audit Office designated by him/her;

k) be empowered to grant the right of representation of the State Audit Office to an officer of the State Audit Office;

l) file a claim with the Constitutional Court of Georgia concerning a dispute over the powers of the State Audit Office;

m) submit accounts, reports, and a conclusion to the Parliament of Georgia;

n) submit to the Parliament of Georgia the budget proposal of the State Audit Office for the subsequent year regarding the budget estimates and the maximum number of the officers;

o) be empowered, and where requested, shall be obliged, to attend meetings of the Parliament of Georgia, a committee of the Parliament of Georgia, a parliamentary faction, and/or an interim commission of inquiry or other interim commissions of the Parliament of Georgia. In addition, upon request, he/she shall be heard by the Parliament of Georgia, a committee of the Parliament of Georgia, a parliamentary faction, and/or an interim commission of inquiry or other interim commissions of the Parliament of Georgia;

p) convene and chair the meetings of the Presidium of the State Audit Office;

q) establish boards, commissions, and working groups;

r) organise the consolidation of evaluations formulated on audit outcomes for the purpose of developing uniform auditing practices;

s) dispose of the funds and material assets of the State Audit Office in accordance with the established procedure;

t) represent the State Audit Office in relations with the governmental authorities of Georgia, relevant authorities of other states, and international and other organisations;

u) exercise other powers granted to him/her by the legislation of Georgia.

### Article 11 - Immunity of the Auditor General

1. The Auditor General shall not be subject to prosecuted, arrest, or detention, nor shall his/her residence, vehicle, workplace, or person be searched, except with the consent of the Parliament of Georgia. An exception shall apply where the Auditor General is apprehended in flagrante delicto, which shall be immediately reported to the Parliament of Georgia. If the Parliament of Georgia does not grant its consent within the next 48 hours, the detained or arrested Auditor General shall be released immediately.

2. Where the Parliament of Georgia grants its consent to the arrest or detention of the Auditor General, his/her powers shall, with the consent of the Parliament of Georgia, be suspended until a decision to terminate the criminal proceedings is passed or until a court judgement enters into legal force.

3. The Auditor General may be removed from office by decision of the Parliament of Georgia, in accordance with Article 48 of the Constitution of Georgia.

4. The grounds for the early termination of powers of the Auditor General shall be the following:

a) a resignation notice;

b) his/her removal from office in accordance with the procedure established by the legislation of Georgia;

c) his/her recognition by a court as a beneficiary of support, unless otherwise determined by a court decision, or recognition as a person with disabilities, or missing, or being declared deceased;

d) holding a position or engaging in activities incompatible with the status of the Auditor General;

e) loss of Georgian citizenship;

f) death.

### Article 12 - Deputy Auditors General

1. The Auditor General shall have deputies, including one First Deputy, who shall be appointed to and dismissed from office by the Auditor General.

2. The rules on the incompatibility of offices established by this Law for the Auditor General shall apply to the Deputy Auditors General.

3. In the event of absence of the Auditor General or his/her inability to exercise his/her powers, the First Deputy Auditor General shall perform his/her duties; whereas, in the event of absence or inability to exercise powers by both the Auditor General and the First Deputy Auditor General, one of the deputies shall perform the duties upon the instruction of the Auditor General.

4. Deputy Auditors General shall be entitled to, and upon request shall be obliged to, attend the sessions of the Parliament of Georgia, parliamentary committees, and/or an interim commission of inquiry or other interim commissions of the Parliament.

5. The First Deputy Auditor General and the deputies shall be in charge of specific fields of activity of the State Audit Office and, in the performance of their official duties, shall be accountable to the Auditor General.

6. Upon the termination of the term of office of the Auditor General, the powers of his/her First Deputy and deputies shall terminate immediately upon the election of a new Auditor General.

b) review and take decisions on complaints concerning audit reports in accordance with Chapter VI of this Law;

## Chapter V - Audit

### Article 17 - Audit Authority of the State Audit Office

1. Within the scope of its competence, the audit authority of the State Audit Office shall extend to:

a) the bodies of legislative, executive and judicial authorities, and municipal bodies;

b) the National Bank of Georgia;

c) legal entities under public law;

d) enterprises established with a 50 per cent or more equity participation by the State, an autonomous republic, and/or a municipality;

e) other legal and natural persons, organisations and institutions.

2. In order to fulfil the objectives determined by the Constitution of Georgia and this Law, the State Audit Office shall audit:

a) the expenditure and execution of the state, autonomous republic, and municipal budgets;

b) the justification and legality of the revenues and payments of the draft state budget and the draft budgets of the autonomous republics;

c) the formation and management of the state debt and issuance of state credits;

d) the legality of the movement of state funds within the National Bank of Georgia and the credit and financial institutions registered in Georgia;

e) the financial and economic activities of the diplomatic service of Georgia;

f) the management and disposal of the state, autonomous republic, and municipal property;

g) the legality of the use of funds allocated for elections, as established by the Organic Law of Georgia the Election Code of Georgia;

h) the expenditure and use of funds and property of legal entities under public law. The State Audit Office shall audit the activities of membership-based legal entities under public law (public law corporations) within the scope of expenditure and use of property and/or funds transferred by the State, an autonomous republic or a municipality. This sub-paragraph shall not apply to legal entities under public law provided for by the Constitutional Agreement of Georgia and national regulatory bodies;

i) the management and use of funds and property of a non-entrepreneurial (non-commercial) legal entity established by the State, an autonomous republic and/or a municipality;

j) the financial and economic activities of a legal entity under private law in which the State, an autonomous republic, a municipality, a legal entity under public law and/or a non-entrepreneurial (non-commercial) legal entity provided for by sub-paragraph (i) of this paragraph hold 50 per cent or more of the shares/stock;

k) information on the financial statements submitted, upon request of the State Audit Office, by a legal entity under private law established with a 50 per cent or more shareholding participation of the State, whose securities – shares or stock – are listed on an international stock exchange;

l) the financial and economic activities of a legal entity under private law in which a legal entity under private law provided for by subparagraph (j) of this paragraph holds 50 per cent or more of the shares/stock;

m) contractual obligations and loans of an entity under private law which are guaranteed by the State, as well as its activities in that part which is related to its use and management of finances belonging to budgetary or state funds, or, in any form, of property owned by the State, an autonomous republic and a municipality;

n)  
the activities of the National Bank of Georgia, the Prosecution Service of Georgia, the State Security Service of Georgia, and the Intelligence Agency within the scope of their competence established by the legislation of Georgia;

o)  
only the administrative and capital expenditures of a national regulatory body;

p) the expenditure of its annual budget by the Legal Entity under Public Law called the Pension Fund of Georgia.

3. The State Audit Office shall independently determine its annual audit activity plan.

### Article 21 - Specialists and experts

1. The State Audit Office shall be authorised to invite and/or involve specialists and experts in the course of an audit. The relationship of the State Audit Office with the invited specialists and experts shall be regulated in accordance with the procedure established by the legislation of Georgia.

2. A specialist or an expert is a person who possesses specialised knowledge and who is invited to conduct a special examination to draw up a relevant act on the circumstances required for the audit.

3. To perform the tasks determined by this article, the rights and duties determined by Article 20 of this Law shall apply to a specialist and an expert, except for the access to the electronic database of administrative bodies provided for by paragraphs 1 and 3(a) of the same article and the rights and duties established by sub-paragraph (c) of the same paragraph.

### Article 23 - Rights and duties of the auditee

1. The auditee shall have the right to:

a) submit comments and opinions during the course of an audit in accordance with the procedure established by the legislation of Georgia;

b) appeal the audit report in accordance with the procedure established by this Law.

2. The auditee shall be obliged to:

a) provide the officers of the State Audit Office with appropriate working conditions, including a workspace;

b) provide the officers of the State Audit Office with all the information required for conducting the audit; the auditee shall be responsible for the authenticity, objectivity and completeness of such information;

## Chapter XI¹- Public Audit Institute

### Article 36¹ - Public Audit Institute

1. The Public Audit Institute ('the Institute') is a legal entity under public law, which is independent in exercising the powers provided for by the legislation of Georgia and the Statute of the Institute.

2. The functions of the Institute shall be: the holding of training sessions on public audit, the certification of public sector auditors ('the certification') and the implementation of continuous compulsory educational programmes, as well as research/analytical, expert, advisory, and public sector auditing services.

6. The service fees of the Institute, as well as the procedure and conditions for their payment, shall be determined by the Institute.

(Shall become effective from 1 January 2030)]

7. The requirements of the Law of Georgia on Public Procurement shall not apply to the procurement of the services of the Institute.

[7. (Deleted – 9.2.2023, No 2583). (Shall become effective from 1 January 2030)]

8. The funds and revenue of the Institute shall be used for the performance of the functions of the Institute and for the development of the system of the State Audit Office.

9. The State Audit Office shall exercise state control over the activities of the Institute.

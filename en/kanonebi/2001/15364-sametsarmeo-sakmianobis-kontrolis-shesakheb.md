# ON CONTROL OF ENTREPRENEURIAL ACTIVITY

LAW OF GEORGIA

ON CONTROL OF ENTREPRENEURIAL ACTIVITY

## Chapter I – General Provisions

### Article 1

1. Everyone has the right to freely carry out entrepreneurial activity.

2. Entrepreneurial activity under the Constitution of Georgia may only be restricted by law.

3. The state, a municipality body, or other administrative body is authorised to exercise control over the activity of an entrepreneur only in accordance with the procedures established by law.

*Law of Georgia No 1697 of 24 September 2009 – LHG I, No 29, 12.10.2009, Art. 180*

*Law of Georgia No 6894 of 15 July 2020 – website, 28.7.2020*

### Article 2

1. Terms used in this Law shall have the following meanings:

a) entrepreneur – an enterprise of any organisational legal structure under the Law of Georgia on Entrepreneurs, as well as a non-entrepreneurial (non- commercial) legal person engaged in entrepreneurial activity as part of this activity;

b) control over entrepreneurial activity – activity of the state bodies, municipality bodies, and other administrative bodies regulated by the legislation of Georgia, which aims to inspect financial and economic activities of entrepreneurs and to verify fulfilment of the obligations imposed on them; to determine if the activities of entrepreneurs comply with the legislation of Georgia; to detect violations in their activities or to impose appropriate sanctions on them. For the purposes of this Law, control shall not be the issuance of a licence, permit or certificate under the legislation of Georgia, or making a decision on authorisation, request of a certificate in the distribution network and drawing up of a respective report; also, activities carried out to verify the fulfilment of international treaties of Georgia in the field of environmental protection, fulfilment of requirements for the use of natural resources, and other environmental requirements, activities under agreements and licences related to oil and gas resources and natural hydrogen resources, inspection determined by Article 8(3) of the Law of Georgia on the Legal Entity under Public Law called the Operative-technical Agency of Georgia, activities carried out to prevent and respond to forced labour and labour exploitation, as well as the facilitation of control over the implementation of decisions made by the administration of the Government of Georgia in accordance with the Law of Georgia on the Structures, Powers and Procedures for the Activities of the Government of Georgia;

c) controlling body – a state, municipality, or other administrative body authorised by law to exercise control over entrepreneurial activities.

2. Taxation bodies and the State Audit Office of Georgia shall exercise control over financial and economic activity of an entrepreneur in cases provided for by the legislation of Georgia and within statutory limits (except for tax relations under the Tax Code of Georgia).

## Chapter II – Procedure for Exercising Control over Entrepreneurial Activity

### Article 3

1. Only a controlling body may exercise control over entrepreneurial activity. If there are appropriate grounds, another state body may make a proposal to a controlling body or court to inspect entrepreneurial activity of an entrepreneur.

2. A controlling body shall be authorised to exercise control over entrepreneurial activity (including, entering an enterprise, requesting documents, suspending its activity, sealing the property of an entrepreneur and inspecting an enterprise and the quality of the production manufactured by the entrepreneur) only by order of the judge.

2¹. In the case provided for in Article 5(1)(i³) or (i⁴) of this Law, after the commencement of control of the entrepreneurial activities by the controlling body in accordance with this Law, the controlling body shall apply to the court and request the issuance by a judge of an order on the control of entrepreneurial activities, specifying the relevant grounds. The procedure for the consideration and resolution by the judge of such matters shall be determined in accordance with Chapter VII¹ of the Administrative Procedure Code of Georgia.

3. A judge shall only issue an order to inspect an entrepreneur's activity when a controlling body submits to him/her appropriate information with well- grounded and reasonable suspicion that the entrepreneur has violated requirements of the legislation.

4. An entrepreneur’s activity shall not be inspected unless a judge issues an order to do so. If the inspection of an entrepreneur is already in progress, it must be terminated immediately after a judge refuses to issue an order on inspection, any sum paid, or other property or document transferred in favour of the controlling body, or the state or municipality budget must be unconditionally returned to the entrepreneur. If this is not possible, the entrepreneur shall be fully and appropriately recompensed. An entrepreneur shall have the right to claim and receive compensation for damage caused by an unauthorised inspection.

5. Evidence that was obtained as a result of an inspection conducted in violation of the law shall be invalid.

6. A controlling body shall be authorised to inspect an entrepreneur only with respect to issues on which there is an order of a judge.

7. The procedure for consideration and solution of an issue under this article by a judge shall be defined under the Administrative Procedure Code of Georgia.

### Article 4

1. If an authorised controlling body has inspected an entrepreneur, it may not be inspected again with respect to the same issue by another controlling body (except as provided for in the second sentence of Article 5(2) of this Law), or by another division of the same controlling body.

2. Before exercising control, a controlling body shall be obliged to provide an entrepreneur with a written list of the entrepreneur’s rights and duties.

h²) activities related to the exercise of control in the area of inland waterway transport, as provided for by the legislation of Georgia, by the Legal Entity under Public Law called the Maritime Transport Agency operating within the system of the Ministry of Economy and Sustainable Development of Georgia;

h³) activities related to the exercise of control as determined by the Law of Georgia on Motor Transport, as well as measures implemented in accordance with the legislation of Georgia in order to supervise the compliance with the applicable technical regulations in the area of motor transport, by the Legal Entity under Public Law called the Land Transport Agency operating within the system of the Ministry of Economy and Sustainable Development of Georgia;

i) verification of satisfaction of technical regulations for high risk medical activities, if the verification is performed on the basis of a random inspection three times in a calendar year;

i³.a) a written complaint from a patient or his/her representative;

i³.b) death of a pregnant woman, a woman who has recently given birth, and/or a newborn baby or a child;

i³.c) an application of an administrative body;

i³.d) severe deterioration of a patient's health condition or his/her unexpected death, which became known through mass media;

i⁴) the control over pharmaceutical activities, related to the pharmaceutical companies and wholesale sales, in accordance with Article 3(2¹) of this Law, by the Legal Entity under Public Law called the Medical and Pharmaceutical Activities Regulation Agency operating under the Ministry of Internally Displaced Persons from the Occupied Territories, Labour, Health and Social Affairs of Georgia, on the following grounds:

i⁴.a) a written complaint from a consumer or his/her representative regarding the quality of a pharmaceutical product;

i⁴.b) an application of an administrative body;

i⁴.c) the disseminated public information according to which:

i⁴.c.a) there is a threat to human life and/or health;

i⁴.c.b) there are circumstances that may be related to the destruction of evidence;

j) activity under the legislation of Georgia carried out by the Legal Entity under Public Law called the National Food Agency within the Ministry of Environmental Protection and Agriculture of Georgia;

k) activity under the law carried out by the Legal Entity under Public Law called the State Insurance Supervision Service of Georgia;

l) activity under the legislation of Georgia carried out by the Emergency Management Service, a state sub-agency within the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Georgia, to supervise facilities under state fire supervision and facilities of vital importance;

m) during carrying out aesthetic and cosmetic procedures in public places by duly authorised persons from municipality bodies:

m.a) checks of the observance of technical regulations – sanitary norms of infection prevention and control in carrying out aesthetic and cosmetic procedures in public places, if random checks are carried out once in a calendar year;

m.b) checks of conditions related to the registration in the registry of economic activities, if random checks are carried out once in a calendar year.2. All controlling bodies shall be prohibited from requiring that an entrepreneur submit information falling outside the scope of their authority. When inspecting other state financial and economic control bodies, the State Audit Office shall be authorised to require that an appropriate body, in order to conduct its desk tax audit, submit documents and information evidencing budget settlements.

n) activities of the Legal Entity under Public Law called the Labour Inspection Office operating under the state control of the Ministry of Internally Displaced Persons from the Occupied Territories, Labour, Health and Social Affairs of Georgia as provided for by the Organic Law of Georgia on Occupational Safety, the Organic Law of Georgia the Labour Code of Georgia, and the Law of Georgia on Labour Inspection Service, except for the case determined by Article 16(2)(b) of the Law of Georgia on Labour Inspection Service;

o) inspection carried out by the State Audit Office of Georgia under the Law of Georgia on the Personal Data Protection.

3. In the cases provided for in paragraph (1)(c)–(f) of this article, control of entrepreneurial activities of an individual legal person may only be exercised based on an administrative act of an appropriate body.

4. This Law shall not apply to actions provided for by the Law of Georgia on Operational Investigations and under the Criminal Procedure Code of Georgia. A controlling body exercising control over entrepreneurial activity at the request of a body conducting operational investigations shall be granted the right of inspection by a judge’s order. The body conducting interrogation and operational investigations shall apply to a court with a motion to conduct an inspection, and shall present appropriate information to the judge on violations of law by the entrepreneur. The judge shall consider the issue alone at a closed judicial session without participation of the parties. The requirements of Article 4(1) of this Law shall not apply to the inspection to be conducted.

### Article 6

Any controlling body shall be obliged, before January 25th of each year, to submit an annual report to the President of Georgia, the Government of Georgia and the Parliament of Georgia on motions submitted to the courts to issue orders, the number of orders issued by courts, and the results of inspections conducted on the basis of the issued orders during the previous year.

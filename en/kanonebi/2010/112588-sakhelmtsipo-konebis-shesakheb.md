8. An individual administrative act issued by the Property Agency shall be appealed to the Ministry or a general court of Georgia, except for a decision made within the framework of exercising the powers of a shareholder/partner of a state-owned enterprise, which is appealed to a general court of Georgia.

### Article 2 – Definition of terms used in this Law

For the purposes of this Law the terms used herein have the following meanings:

a) state property – movable and immovable things and intangible assets owned by the state;

b) administration of state property – the transfer of title of state property to a municipality, and the privatisation, transfer of management rights, exchange, lending out in the form of leasing, distribution and destruction of state property under the procedure established by the legislation of Georgia;

c) privatisation – the acquisition of title to state property by natural and legal persons or by their associations as provided for by this Law by electronic and/or public auctions, direct sale, competitive direct sale and the transfer of title free of charge, as well as through a trade outlet or a third person, and also by the acquisition of an ownership interest or shares or share certificates directly or through an intermediary, by public or private offer or any other form of offer corresponding to the practice established at a recognised foreign stock exchange or in international capital markets for the given period;

c¹) a recognised foreign stock exchange – a stock exchange entered on the list of stock exchanges recognised by the National Bank of Georgia under Article 2(62) of the Law of Georgia on the Securities Market;

d) transfer of state property for use – a transfer of state property with the right to use as provided for by the legislation of Georgia;

e) transferor of state property for use – a property administrator that transfers state property for use on behalf of the State;

f) transfer of management rights – a transfer of the right to manage a state-owned ownership interest and shares (ownership interest and shares) to natural or legal persons or to other entities;

g) property transferred into state ownership – property transferred into state ownership through the criminal, civil or administrative proceedings of Georgia, property transferred (transferred in kind) into state ownership under the Law of Georgia on Enforcement Proceedings, and property transferred into (passed to) state ownership under the Tax Code of Georgia, property transferred into (passed to) state ownership under the Customs Code of Georgia, property transferred into (passed to) state ownership under the Law of Georgia on Weapons, and property transferred into state ownership by inheritance or donation, property subject to privatisation, and ownerless or heirless property transferred into state ownership, and unreturned goods after a test purchase under the Tax Code of Georgia;

h) distribution of state-owned movable property – a gratuitous transfer of movable property in state ownership in accordance with the legislation of Georgia;

i) destruction of state-owned movable property – destruction of movable property in state ownership in accordance with the legislation of Georgia;

i¹) property transferred for administration – state-owned movable property which has been transferred for use to and/or is on the books of a state body/a legal entity under public law, and which was transferred to the Property Agency for further administration after the decision on its administration was made;

j) third person – a natural or legal person or an association of persons that facilitates the privatisation of state property;

k) (Deleted – 11.11.2025, No 1023);

l) ownership interest and shares – ownership interests and shares registered with an appropriate registration authority in the name of the State;

m) initial transfer for use – the transfer for use by a property administrator of state property that has not been transferred for use to a body of state authority of Georgia, or of the Autonomous Republic of Abkhazia, or of the Autonomous Republic of Ajara, or to a municipality, or to natural and legal persons;

n) (Deleted – 22.5.2020, No 5960);

o) (Deleted – 29.5.2020, No 5989);

p) state-owned unleased agricultural land plot – a state-owned agricultural land plot that has not been leased as of 25 January 2005;

q) state-owned leased agricultural land plot – a state-owned agricultural land plot that is the subject of a lease agreement;

r) (Deleted – 22.5.2020, No 5960);

s) (deleted);

t) immovable thing – state-owned immovable property, agricultural land, forests and forest lands, protected landscape and/or multiple-use areas;

t¹) immovable property – a state-owned non-agricultural land plot with or without a building on it (under construction, completed or demolished), a building unit (under construction, completed or demolished), a line structure, or perennial plants on the land;

t²) agricultural land – an agricultural land plot of pastureland, grassland, arable land (including those covered with perennial plants) or homestead land category, which is used or can be used for agricultural purposes, with or without structures or buildings on it;

u) unconditional and irrevocable bank guarantee – an unconditional and irrevocable bank guarantee to be submitted for the administration of state property;

v) deposit – the amount of money to be paid as a security deposit into the account specified by the entity conducting privatisation in the form of an auction or in any other form; the freezing of the money in the bank account of the participant shall be treated as equivalent to a deposit, which will be transferred to the entity conducting the auction if the participant wins the auction or violates the procedure and conditions established by this Law;

w) (Deleted – 29.5.2020, No 5989);

x) privatisation proceeds – the amount of money earned from the privatisation of state property/the transfer of state property with the management rights and from its exchange, which is transferred to the state budget of Georgia, the republican budgets of the Autonomous Republics of Abkhazia and Ajara and/or the municipal budgets;

y) privatisation price – the amount of money to be paid by the buyer of state property or by the acquirer of management rights to state property, which consists of the privatisation amount and the amount payable to a third person and/or to another entity (if any);

z) initial privatisation amount – the minimum cost of the state property upon the privatisation of or the transfer of management rights to such property, the price of which is raised during the bidding;

z1) (Deleted – 29.5.2020, No 5989);

z2) service fee – the sum of money determined for the services provided by the Property Agency, including for facilitation of the administration of property of the interested natural persons and legal entities under public and private law, which is fully transferred to the account of the Property Agency;

z3) privatisation amount – the amount of money that consists of the privatisation proceeds and the payments to the Property Agency (if any);

z4) initial price of transfer for use – in the case of transfer of state property for use, the minimum amount of the property subject to transfer for use, at which the state property is made available for transfer for use and the price of which is raised during the bidding;

z5) price of transfer for use – in the case of the transfer of the property for use, the amount of money to be paid by the beneficiary for the use of the property, which consists of the amount of the transfer of the property for use and the payments to the Property Agency (if any);

z6) payments to the Property Agency – a portion of the privatisation amount or the price of transfer for use determined by the Government of Georgia, which will be transferred to the account of the Property Agency in the case of privatisation or transfer of management rights or transfer for use by the Property Agency; and a portion of the payments specified in Article 49¹(2) of this Law that is transferred to the account of the Property Agency in the amount determined by the Government of Georgia; and the amount of money determined by an ordinance of the Government of Georgia for the services provided by the Property Agency;

z7) amount of the transfer for use – the amount of money payable to the respective budget in the case of transfer of property for use;

z7¹) property administrator – the Ministry, the Property Agency or a body acting within the scope of powers delegated thereby, or a person using state property (except for a natural person and a legal entity under private law), which exercises the powers provided for by this Law under the procedure and in the manner defined by an ordinance of the Government of Georgia.

### Article 3 – Acquirer of state property

1. An acquirer of state property (except for cases of privatisation of a state-owned agricultural land plot and of state-owned movable property) may be a citizen of Georgia or a foreign citizen, a legal entity under private law or an association of such entities, in the property of which the participatory interest of a state body or a municipality/municipalities is less than 25%, a non-entrepreneurial (non-commercial) legal entity established by the State or another entity, or jointly established by the State and another entity, the National Bank of Georgia, a national regulatory body, the Public Broadcaster, and the Georgian Apostolic Autocephalous Orthodox Church in the case of a direct sale of state property by decision of the Government of Georgia.

1¹. Any natural or legal person, an association of persons, a state body, or a municipality may be an acquirer of state-owned movable property.

2. The privatisation of a state-owned agricultural land plot shall be possible for a fee for citizens of Georgia or legal entities under private law registered in Georgia, considering the requirements defined by the Organic Law of Georgia on Agricultural Land Ownership, and the Law of Georgia on Determination of the Designated Purpose of Land and Sustainable Management of Agricultural Land, and free of charge for the Georgian Apostolic Autocephalous Orthodox Church and for those homeless citizens of Georgia who have lived or live in the occupied territories of Georgia.

3. By a decision of the Government of Georgia, the procedure and conditions for the transfer of state-owned unleased agricultural land plots in the form of a gratuitous privatisation to the homeless citizens of Georgia who have lived or live in the occupied territories of Georgia shall be determined by an ordinance of the Government of Georgia. The relevant property administrator shall prepare and submit the issue to the Government of Georgia for the making of a decision.

4. (Deleted).

5. The Property Agency shall, with the consent of the Government of Georgia, exchange state property (except for movable property) for property owned by a natural person, a legal entity under private law, the National Bank of Georgia, and by the Georgian Apostolic Autocephalous Orthodox Church. The procedure and conditions for exchanging this state property shall be determined by an Order of the Minister of Economy and Sustainable Development of Georgia. A state-owned immovable thing may be exchanged in cases provided for by an ordinance of the Government of Georgia.

6. Where so provided for by paragraph 1 of this article, on the basis of an appropriate application of the Georgian Apostolic Autocephalous Orthodox Church and on the motion of the Ministry of Environmental Protection and Agriculture of Georgia, areas of forests adjacent to the Orthodox Churches or Monasteries available before 2021, but not more than 20 hectares in each case, and the assigned areas defined by the Forest Code of Georgia, may be transferred into the ownership of the Georgian Apostolic Autocephalous Orthodox Church. In addition, the area to be transferred must be defined so that its allotment does not violate the boundaries of the available alphabetical designation of forests.

### Article 3¹ – Procedure for entering into a transaction upon the privatisation of a state-owned immovable thing

1. In the case of the privatisation of a state-owned immovable thing, an appropriate agreement shall be entered into between the agency conducting the privatisation and the acquirer of the property, which shall be the basis for the registration of the title to the property with the Public Registry and for the creation of appropriate obligations (including the obligation to pay the privatisation price, if any).

2. In the case of the privatisation of a state-owned immovable thing by an unconditional auction, the form of the appropriate agreement shall be approved by the Ministry. The agency conducting the privatisation shall issue a winning bid confirmation in a written and/or electronic form.

3. In the case of privatisation of a state-owned immovable thing by auction, an appropriate agreement shall be concluded within a period of 45 calendar days after the auction is held. In the case of privatisation of a state-owned immovable thing by the Government of Georgia by way of direct sale, an appropriate agreement shall be concluded within a period of three months after the related act is adopted by the Government of Georgia. If the appropriate agreement is not concluded within the period provided for by this paragraph, the auction results/the act (decision) of the Government of Georgia shall lose effect after the expiry of the period provided for by the same paragraph.

4. The purchaser shall pay the privatisation price within the time limits determined by the agency conducting the privatisation, which must not be less than seven days and not more than two calendar years after the auction, and shall submit to the agency conducting the privatisation a document confirming the payment (the obligation to submit a document confirming the payment of the privatisation price shall not apply to cases where property is transferred for administration or use by electronic auction).

5. Within 30 calendar days after the submission of a document confirming the fulfilment of the obligation, the entity administering the state property shall issue a written or electronic confirmation of the fulfilment of the obligation in full or in part, which is the basis for registration with the Public Registry and for the discharge from or change of relevant obligations.

6. Where there is consent of a property administrator, the property acquirer shall have the right to mortgage the acquired immovable thing in order to fulfil the privatisation obligation/obligations.

7. Except as provided for by paragraph 6 of this article, where there is consent of a property administrator, the property acquirer shall have the right to mortgage the acquired immovable thing, unless the fulfilment of the privatisation obligation/obligations, based on the content of the obligation/obligations, requires making of an investment/payment of expenses.

8. In the case of mortgaging state-administered property under paragraphs 6 and 7 of this article, upon the transfer of the mortgaged property into the ownership of the mortgagee in the case of the sale (irrespective of the form of sale) of the mortgaged property, under the Law of Georgia on Enforcement Proceedings or under the Civil Code of Georgia, due to the failure of the mortgagor to meet the mortgagee's claims secured by the mortgage, the privatisation obligations undertaken by the acquirer of the property before the State and the rights and duties originating from the relevant agreement shall be fully transferred to the new and each subsequent owner if the relevant information on the privatisation obligation (obligations) is registered in the records on the immovable thing with the Public Registry before the property is mortgaged.

9. Paragraph 8 of this article shall not apply to the state property administered within the public and private partnership project provided for by the Law of Georgia on Public-Private Partnerships.

### Article 3² – Exchange

1. The rules provided for by Articles 3¹(5-8) and 20 of this Law shall apply to the exchange of state-owned movable things.

2. The rules provided for by Articles 3¹(5), 20, 42(6-8) and 45²(7-9) of this Law shall apply when exchanging state-owned intangible property.

3. During exchange, an appropriate agreement shall be concluded within a period of three months after the related decision is made by the Government of Georgia. If the related agreement is not concluded within the period provided for by this paragraph, the decision of the Government of Georgia shall lose effect after expiry of the period provided for by the same paragraph.

*Law of Georgia No 5989 of 29 May 2020 – website, 10.6.2020*

### Article 3³ – Sale of property administered by the State through compulsory enforcement

Upon the sale of property administered by the State in accordance with the procedure established by the Law of Georgia on Enforcement Proceedings prior to the full performance of privatisation conditions (regardless of the form of sale of that property), the privatisation obligation/obligations undertaken by the acquirer of the property towards the State, and the rights and duties arising from the relevant contract, shall be transferred in full to the new owner and to each subsequent owner, provided that the relevant information reflecting the privatisation obligation/obligations has been registered with the appropriate registering body before the sale of the said property under the procedure established by this Article.

### Article 4 – State property not subject to privatisation

1. The following state property shall not be subject to privatisation:

a) the subsoil;

b) water resources, the river bed, and the natural lake bed;

c) territorial waters;

d) the continental shelf;

e) the state forest, except as provided for by Article 3(6) of this Law;

f) the airspace;

g) state preserves;

h) national parks;

i) natural monuments;

j) sanctuaries;

j¹) (Deleted – 29.5.2020, No 5989);

k) cultural values, cultural property, land plots within the area of archeologic protection, and cultural and artistic structures or buildings, and land plots on which such structures or buildings are located, without the appropriate conditions and agreement with the Ministry of Culture of Georgia;

l) religious buildings and places of worship (functioning and no longer functioning), and their ruins, as well as the land plots on which they are located;

m) historical and cultural state archives;

n) the state holding of cinema, photo and phono documents;

o) archives and collections of the ministries/agencies, and scientific and research institutions of Georgia, or documents of the national archives holding (movable property);

p) museum collections and holdings;

q) monuments included in the World Heritage List and house-museums of national importance;

r) dispatching facilities of the energy sector;

s) property used by legal entities under public law and in which public schools and higher educational and scientific institutions of Georgia are operating;

t) berths, hydraulic structures, lighthouses, beacons and water areas of ports of special importance;

u) motorways (in the absence of roads of alternative importance);

v) means of air traffic, management and control;

x) the frequency spectrum;

z) state pantheons;

z2) the following state-owned agricultural land:

## Chapter VIII – State-owned Intangible Assets

### Article 39 – State-owned intangible assets

1. State-owned intangible assets shall include ownership interests and shares, as well as all the claims and rights that may be transferred to other persons, or that are intended to provide the owner of the assets with material benefit and/or with the right to make claims against other persons.

2. Under the Law of Georgia on Entrepreneurs, the property administrator may establish joint stock companies and limited liability companies and/or non-entrepreneurial (non-commercial) legal entities on the basis of the state property.

3. In an enterprise, in which the State holds more than 50% of the total number of votes, the Supervisory Board may be established by decision of the Government of Georgia, except as provided for by paragraphs 3¹ and 3² of this article. A state representative in the Supervisory Board of the said enterprise, and in the Supervisory Board of  
an enterprise established with 50% or more state participation,  
may be a public servant unless he/she has a conflict of interests with that enterprise. A member of the Supervisory Board of an enterprise who is simultaneously a public servant shall perform the duties imposed on him/her without additional remuneration, and his/her activities shall not be considered an incompatibility of offices in a public institution.

3¹. By decision of the Property Agency, the Supervisory Board may be established in a state-owned enterprise engaged in electricity and natural gas production and trade. Requirements provided for by the Law of Georgia on Energy and Water Supply must be observed in relation to the Supervisory Board of the enterprise and its members. A state representative in the Supervisory Board of the said enterprise may be a public servant unless he/she has a conflict of interests with that enterprise. A member of the Supervisory Board of an enterprise who is simultaneously a public servant shall perform the duties imposed on him/her without additional remuneration, and his/her activities shall not be considered an incompatibility of offices in a public institution.

3². Establishment of the Supervisory Board in an enterprise operating as a state-owned electricity transmission system operator and natural gas transmission system operator shall be compulsory. Requirements provided for by the Law of Georgia on Energy and Water Supply must be observed in relation to the Supervisory Board of the enterprise and its members. A state representative in the Supervisory Board of the said enterprise may be a public servant unless he/she has a conflict of interests with that enterprise. A member of the Supervisory Board of an enterprise who is simultaneously a public servant shall perform the duties imposed on him/her without additional remuneration, and his/her activities shall not be considered an incompatibility of offices in a public institution. The majority of members of the Supervisory Board of a state-owned electricity transmission system operator and natural gas transmission system operator must not be public servants.

4. A decision on the acquisition/redemption of shares of an enterprise operating with state participation, in accordance with the procedure established by the Law of Georgia on Entrepreneurs, shall be made by the Government of Georgia.

5. A state-owned electricity transmission system operator and natural gas transmission system operator shall be administered by the relevant department of the Ministry. The said department shall not define/develop the energy policy, including it shall not elaborate/set the relevant regulatory standards.

### Article 42 – Privatisation of ownership interests and shares

### Article 49 – Change of state property privatisation conditions, exemption from sanctions imposed/to be imposed for violation of privatisation conditions

In relation to the purchasers of state property that applied to the Ministry not later than 1 July 2010 to lift the sanctions imposed for the violation of other obligations undertaken under the relevant record/agreement and/or to change/review obligations, the Ministry may, for the purpose of promoting investment activities, and taking into account public and private interests, and in the case of justified necessity, make a decision, with the consent of the Government of Georgia, on changing/reviewing the conditions of a record/agreement entered into for the privatisation of state property privatised by a previous form of auction or privatisation, or the conditions of a purchase agreement entered into for the privatisation of state property privatised by direct sale (except for the privatisation conditions established by the President of Georgia), and/or on lifting any sanctions imposed for the non-fulfilment of obligations. If there is a request to change/review the privatisation conditions established by the President of Georgia, the Ministry shall review the application of the purchaser of the state property and refer the issue, with an appropriate proposal (draft legal act) to the Government of Georgia, which may, for the purpose of promoting investment activities, and taking into account public and private interests, and in the case of justified necessity, submit to the President of Georgia a proposal on changing/reviewing the privatisation conditions established by the President of Georgia for the state property privatised by direct sale. A decision on changing/reviewing the privatisation conditions established by the President of Georgia for state property shall be made by the President of Georgia.

### Article 49¹– Change/annulment of the condition of privatisation/transfer into ownership/transfer with the right of management/transfer for use of state property and/or remission of a penalty imposed/to be imposed under the agreement

1. In those contractual relationships where one of the parties is the Property Agency and/or the Ministry, a party to the agreement may submit a reasoned request to the property administrator to change/annul a condition of privatisation/transfer into ownership/transfer with the right of management/transfer for use of state property, and/or to remit an unpaid penalty imposed/to be imposed under the agreement. The property administrator shall review the submitted request and, taking into consideration public and private interests, shall submit a proposal to the Government of Georgia for the making of a decision on changing/annulling the condition of privatisation/transfer into ownership/transfer with the right of management/transfer for use of the state property, and/or on remitting the unpaid penalty imposed/to be imposed under the agreement.

2. In the case specified in paragraph 1 of this article, based on an application of the property administrator and by a decision of the Government of Georgia, the acquirer of the state property/the person receiving the right of management/use, may be required to make an appropriate payment in return for changing/annulling the condition of privatisation/transfer into ownership/transfer with the right of management/transfer for use of the state property; the amount of this payment shall be determined by the Government of Georgia. The payment shall be transferred to the state budget of Georgia, except for the payment to the Property Agency, which shall be transferred to the account of the Property Agency in the amount determined by the Government of Georgia.

3. In the case of privatisation/transfer for use of state property by auction after 31 December 2012, this article shall not apply to the change of the amount of the privatisation price/the fee for transfer for use and of the time limit for its payment, and to the remission of the penalty imposed/to be imposed for the failure to comply with the obligation to pay the privatisation price/the fee for transfer for use.

3¹. If the condition of privatisation/transfer into ownership/transfer with the right of management/transfer for use of the state property is changed/annulled, the related agreement shall be concluded within 3 months after the adoption of an appropriate decree by the Government of Georgia. If the related agreement is not concluded within the period provided for by this paragraph, the decree of the Government of Georgia shall lose effect after the expiry of the period provided for by the same paragraph.

4. The citizens of Georgia to whom the state property has been transferred into ownership by privatisation by direct sale based on their status as an internally displaced person shall be exempted from the financial liabilities originating from before 1 July 2014 under the state property purchase agreement, including the obligation to pay a penalty imposed/to be imposed.

### Article 49²– Exemption from payment of the fee imposed/to be imposed for unlawful use of state property

Natural and legal persons shall be exempted from the obligation to pay the fee for the unlawful use of state property for entrepreneurial activities (commercial purposes) under Article 36(1³) of this Law, and the price of the transfer of the state property for use payable for the period of use up to 17 September 2012. If the title to state property is registered with the Public Registry after 17 September 2012, natural and legal persons shall be exempted from the payment of the fee for unlawful use of state property for entrepreneurial activities (commercial purposes) under Article 36(1³) of this Law for the period up to the registration of the title to state property with the Public Registry.

*Law of Georgia No 2878 of 11 December 2014 – website, 23.12.2014*

### Article 49³– Measures to be carried out in relation to a party of contractual relationship (contracting party) in connection with a state of emergency, an epidemic or pandemic especially dangerous for public health

1. If the compliance with the condition of privatisation/transfer into ownership/transfer with the right of management/transfer for use of the state property is impossible or impeded during a state of emergency, an epidemic or pandemic  
especially dangerous for public health,  
a decision regarding the measures to be carried out in relation to a party of contractual relationship concerned and/or the making of changes to an agreement (except for the reduction of the privatisation price) on the basis of an appropriate application filed to a property administrator by that party (person) shall be made by the Government of Georgia upon recommendation of the property administrator, in accordance with Article 398 of the Civil Code of Georgia.

2. The restriction imposed under Article 49¹(3) of this Law in the case provided for by paragraph 1 of this article shall not apply.

3. The Government of Georgia shall be empowered to specify the individual cases when decisions regarding the issues provided for by paragraph 1 of this article are to be made by the property administrator.

### Article 49⁴– Temporary procedure for exempting from an unpaid penalty imposed/a penalty to be imposed for the violation of the condition of privatisation/the condition of transfer for use of state property within the administrative boundaries of Tbilisi city Municipality, and/or for changing this condition

1. On the basis of the commissioning of a facility provided for by Article 141³ of the Code of Georgia on Spatial Planning, Architectural and Construction Activities, or Article 103³ of the Product Safety and Free Movement Code, an acquirer of state property/a person receiving state property with the right of use shall be exempted from payment of an unpaid penalty imposed/a penalty to be imposed for the violation of the condition of privatisation/the condition of transfer for use of state property, which has been caused by the exceeding of the period of commissioning of the facility.

2. The decision regarding the change of the condition of privatisation/the condition of transfer for use of state property, which has been caused by the exceeding of the period of commissioning of a facility provided for by Article 141³ of the Code of Georgia on Spatial Planning, Architectural and Construction Activities, or Article 103³ of the Product Safety and Free Movement Code, shall be made by the Government of Georgia under the procedure established by Article 49¹(3) of this Law. In such a case, the restriction imposed by Article 49¹(3) of this Law shall not apply.

### Article 50 – Repealed normative acts

Upon the entry of this Law into force, the following laws shall be deemed void:

b) the Law of Georgia of 30 May 1997 on the Privatisation of State Property, Privatisation of the Property of Local Self-government Units and their Transfer for Use (the Gazette of the Parliament of Georgia, No 29-30, 9.7.1997, p. 15);

d) the Law of Georgia of 3 November 2009 on the Transfer of State-owned Property for Use(the Legislative Herald of Georgia, N 35, 19.11.2009, Art. 245).

# On Social Security of Persons Transferred to the Reserve from Military Bodies, Internal Affairs Bodies and the Special State Protection Service, and Their Family Members

LAW OF GEORGIA

ON SOCIAL SECURITY OF PERSONS TRANSFERRED TO THE RESERVE FROM MILITARY BODIES AND THE SPECIAL STATE PROTECTION SERVICE AND THEIR FAMILY MEMBERS

*Law of Georgia No 1887 of 25 December 2002 - LHG I, No 3, 17.1.2003, Art. 15*

*Law of Georgia No 2459 of 23 December 2005 - LHG I, No 56, 28.12.2005, Art. 401*

*Law of Georgia No 4099 of 3 April 2024 – website, 18.4.2024*

## Chapter I - General Provisions

### Article 1 - Persons entitled to state compensation

According to the conditions, provisions and procedures established by this Law, the following persons having the citizenship of Georgia, transferred to the reserve from military bodies and the Special State Protection Service shall be entitled to state compensation:

a) officers, service members accepted into extended voluntary military service and into the first rank of military reserve on a contractual basis, who have served in the Georgian military/defence forces, the internal forces of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Georgia, the border forces of the State Border Defence Department of Georgia, state security bodies, the State Intelligence Department, the Intelligence Service of Georgia, the system of the State Security Service of Georgia, the office of the National Security Council of Georgia; also service members, including conscripts serving in the National Military Service and persons with special state ranks who had served in the system of the Ministry of Defence of Georgia;

b) persons with a special rank, who have served in the Special State Protection Service;

c) officers, including officers of the Military Prosecutor's Office, former Military Tribunal and military court; service members accepted into extended voluntary military service on a contractual basis, who have served in the former USSR and CIS armed forces, internal, border, railway, government communications, civil defence and military civil construction forces, in national security, foreign intelligence and military justice bodies, in other legitimate military formations and the armed forces of CIS countries, and their family members, unless the issue of pension provision of these persons is otherwise determined by the treaties or international agreements of Georgia;

d) partisans, volunteers, commanders of partisan and volunteer detachments, whose positions corresponded to those of officers;

e) family members of military personnel who died in World War II and other armed conflicts, went missing, or died as a result of wounds, mutilations, contusions, or other diseases received in the wars and conflicts;

f) family members of deceased military pensioners;

g) military service members of the armed forces of other states, employees of internal affairs and state security bodies and their family members - on the basis of treaties (agreements) concluded between Georgia and these states.

### Article 2 - Bases for entitlement to state compensation

The bases for entitlement to state compensation are:

a) the expiry of the term of service established by law

b) the attainment of the age of 65

c) the establishment of a disability status

d) the death of a breadwinner.

*Law of Georgia No 2459 of 23 December 2005 - LHG I, No 56, 28.12.2005, Art. 401*

### Article 3 - Exercise of the right of awarding state compensations

1. Service members who are entitled to state compensation according to this Law shall receive state compensation after transfer to the reserve or resignation.

2. Service members shall be awarded state compensation due to disability notwithstanding the length of service.

3. The conditions of awarding of state compensation or state pension to family members of deceased service members are regulated by the Law of Georgia on State Compensation and State Academic Stipends or the Law of Georgia on State Pensions.

4. State compensation shall not be awarded to persons deprived of military or special ranks by a court and their family members, except if they have been acquitted or rehabilitated according to the procedures established by the legislation of Georgia.

## Chapter II - State Compensation for Service Members

*Law of Georgia No 2459 of 23 December 2005 - LHG I, No 56, 28.12.2005, Art. 401*

### Article 11 - Conditions defining entitlement of service members to state compensation

State compensation shall be awarded to:

a) service members listed in Article 1 of this Law who, by the date of discharge from military service, have served 20 or more calendar years, as well as service members who were forcedly discharged in 1956 during the dissolution of the Georgian military formation (division) and served 10 or more calendar years in the armed forces and have the total length of service of 20 or more calendar years; as well as service members of the Armed Forces of Georgia who started military service from 1991 to 1995 and were discharged due to age limit, served at least 10 calendar years in the armed forces and have the total length of service of 20 or more calendar years;

b) service members who, due to particularly significant state need, were called up for military service, have been in military service by the date of attaining a retirement age and have served 10 years in military/defence forces;

c) pensioners discharged from military service in the armed forces of the former USSR, in internal affairs bodies and state security bodies, border forces, and other legitimate military formations, who were subsequently enrolled in regular military service of the Georgian military/defence forces as senior officer personnel and higher officer personnel.

### Article 16 - Calculation of the length of service

1. For the purpose of awarding state compensations, the length of service shall include: military service, service in the bodies of Prosecutor's Office; service in internal affairs bodies and state security bodies; service in the office of the National Security Council; service in the system of the Emergency Management Service, a special-purpose state institution directly subordinate to the Prime Minister of Georgia; service in the Special State Protection Service; service in the Intelligence Service of Georgia; service in the system of the State Security Service of Georgia; service with the special state rank at the Ministry of Defence of Georgia, service in guerrilla armies and formations; the period of work in state authorities and other state organisations: military service, or regular service in internal affairs bodies and state security bodies and the Special State Protection Service; periods of captivity, unless the captive had committed a crime against the State; the period of service of a sentence and the period of detention, provided that a service member, a private or a senior officer had been unjustly prosecuted or repressed.

2. For service members discharged from military service, the period of study in a civil, special or higher institution before being enrolled in military service in internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, the State Intelligence Department, the Intelligence Service of Georgia; the system of the State Security Service of Georgia, the Special State Protection Service, and the period of study in a civil, special or higher institutions before being enrolled with the special state rank within the system of the Ministry of Defence of Georgia shall be included in the length of service. 1 year of study over 6 years shall be counted as 6 months.

## Chapter III - State Compensation for Service Members having a Disability Status

*Law of Georgia No 2459 of 23 December 2005 - LHG I, No 56, 28.12.2005, Art. 401*

### Article 17 - Conditions of awarding state compensation to service members having a disability status

Service members listed in Article 1 of this Law shall be awarded state compensation due to disability if they became disabled during the period of military or operative service, or not later than three months after discharge from the service, or later than this term due to wounds, contused wounds, injuries or disease.

## Chapter IV - State Compensation for Family Members of Deceased Service Members

### Article 25 - Conditions of awarding state compensation to family members of deceased service members

1. State compensation shall be awarded to family members of service members referred to in Article 1 of this Law if the service member died or was killed during military service, or not later than three months after retirement from the military service, or later due to other disease that the service member suffered from during military service or operative service. This right shall be exercised by family members of former prisoners of war who did not surrender voluntarily and who during captivity did not commit any action directed against the homeland.

2. The family of a service member recognised as missing during hostility on the basis of a court decision shall be equal to a family of a service member killed in the war.

*Law of Georgia No 1819 of 3 December 2002 - LHG I, No 32, 20.12.2002, Art. 158*

*Law of Georgia No 2561 of 23 July 2003 - LHG I, No 24, 20.8.2003, Art. 182*

### Article 26 - Family members entitled to state compensation due to the death of a service member

The following persons shall be entitled to state compensation due to the death of a service member:

a) children until their attainment of the age of 18

b) a disabled spouse, parents and children.

*Law of Georgia No 2459 of 23 December 2005 - LHG I, No 56, 28.12.2005, Art. 401*

### Article 59 - Considering the length of service in awarding state compensation to persons of certain categories in the case of their discharge from service

1. The years of previous service in the armed forces, defence forces of Georgia and law enforcement bodies (police) by officers of the Military Prosecutor's Office and military courts, as well as other employees of the bodies of the Prosecutor's Office of Georgia, shall be included in the total length of service. In the case of discharge from service, their status shall be equal to the status of military officers and police officers.

2. Persons, who have done extended and contracted service in the Ministry of Defence of Georgia and the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Georgia, the system of the State Security Service of Georgia and the Special State Protection Service of Georgia, shall also exercise the rights defined in paragraph 1 of this article if they were transferred from one above-mentioned agency to another.
